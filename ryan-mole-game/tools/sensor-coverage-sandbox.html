Person width matters: the map treats a location as covered if any part of a person that wide standing there intersects a beam cone. Set it to 0 to see the harsh point-target picture, 30 for a realistic torso/legs. HC-SR04 datasheets quote a 15° total measuring angle (7.5° half-angle); soft clothing at long range behaves closer to the narrow end, hard targets wider. Coverage percentages are computed on a 1 cm grid; the gap readout is the widest across-the-lane dead strip anywhere in Box 1.
